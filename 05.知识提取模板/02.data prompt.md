## Notes on Numerical Extraction

1. **Scientific Notation**: Uniformly use `× 10ⁿ` or `e` format
2. **Unit Consistency**:
   - Conductivity: S cm⁻¹
   - Concentration: M (mol/L) or mol%
   - Loading: mg cm⁻²
   - Capacity: mAh g⁻¹ or mAh cm⁻²
3. **Temperature Representation**: Uniformly use °C or K
4. **Chemical Formulas**: Use correct subscripts (e.g., LiTFSI, LiPF₆, NCM811)

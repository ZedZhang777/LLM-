## Research Background and Motivation

This research addresses critical challenges in solid-state polymer electrolytes (SPEs) for high-voltage lithium metal batteries, including low ionic conductivity at room temperature, low Li⁺ transference number (tLi⁺), poor high-voltage stability, instability with lithium metal anodes (LMAs), and high flammability. Current polycarbonate-based SPEs suffer from difficulties in lithium salt dissociation, low tLi⁺, and remain flammable despite mitigating liquid electrolyte leakage risks. The study aims to develop a multifunctional SPE that simultaneously achieves excellent lithium salt dissociation, high tLi⁺, stable LMA performance, high-voltage stability, and superior flame retardancy through a facile and scalable preparation strategy.

## Research Methods and Innovation Points

### Composition of Gel Electrolyte

| Component | Specific Material | Content/Ratio |
|---------|---------|----------|
| **Polymer Matrix/Monomer** | Vinyl ethylene carbonate (VEC), 2,2,3,3,4,4,4-heptafluorobutyl methacrylate (HFBMA), triallyl phosphate (TAP) | n(VEC):n(HFBMA):n(TAP)=1:0.1:0.15 |
| **Lithium Salt** | Lithium bis(trifluoromethanesulfonyl)imide (LiTFSI) | 20 wt% (optimized) |
| **Solvent** | None (solid-state system) | - |
| **Additive** | AIBN initiator | 1.0 wt% |
| **Initiator/Crosslinking Agent** | Thermal radical initiator (AIBN) | 1.0 wt% relative to monomers |
| **Polymerization Conditions** | 65°C for 2h (pre-polymerization) + 70°C for 6h (final polymerization) | In-situ free radical polymerization |

### Polymerization Method

- **Polymerization Type**: In-situ free radical polymerization
- **Polymerization Conditions**: 65°C for 2h pre-polymerization followed by 70°C for 6h final polymerization, initiated by AIBN thermal decomposition

### Key Innovation Points

1. **Puzzle-like Molecular Assembly Strategy**: Novel approach combining three functional molecular segments (VEC for Li⁺ conduction, HFBMA for high-voltage stability and SEI formation, TAP for flame retardancy and anion affinity) through facile in-situ polymerization.

2. **Dual-functionality Design**: Simultaneous enhancement of anion affinity (TAP segments) to restrict TFSI⁻ migration and promotion of lithium salt dissociation, resulting in ultra-high Li⁺ transference number (0.70) and improved ionic conductivity.

3. **Intrinsic Safety Integration**: Incorporation of phosphorus-rich TAP segments provides superior fire-retardant properties through H· and OH· radical trapping, achieving non-flammable SPE while maintaining excellent electrochemical performance.

### Special Performance

- **Wide-Temperature Performance**: Stable operation from room temperature to 100°C, with 120 cycles maintained at 100°C vs failure of PVEC after 2 cycles
- **Safety**: Superior non-flammability (retains shape after flame exposure), passes nail penetration test without thermal runaway, 55.5°C higher triggering temperature (T₂) in ARC tests vs liquid electrolyte
- **High Specific Energy**: 2.6-Ah pouch cell achieves 349 Wh kg⁻¹ specific energy based on total pouch cell weight
- **Mechanical Robustness**: Withstands twisting, cutting, and penetration without leakage or combustion

## Mechanism Analysis and Characterization

The study employed comprehensive characterization techniques to elucidate the superior performance mechanisms:

1. **Molecular Dynamics Simulations**: Revealed weaker Li⁺-TFSI⁻ association (CN reduced from 0.41 to 0.21) and stronger TFSI⁻-polymer interaction (binding energy increased from -1.51 to -2.11 eV), explaining the high tLi⁺ (0.70). Lower LiTFSI dissociation energy (0.76 vs 2.97 eV) in IWSWN-SPE.

2. **Spectroscopic Analysis**: ⁷Li ssNMR showed increased Li⁺ mobility (upfield shift), Raman confirmed higher LiTFSI dissociation, and ¹⁹F/³¹P ssNMR verified incorporation of HFBMA and TAP segments.

3. **Electron Microscopy and XPS**: Cryo-TEM revealed dual-layer SEI with inorganic inner layer (LiF, Li₂CO₃, LiOH, LiPO₃, Li₂O) and amorphous outer layer. Uniform elemental distribution confirmed by EDS. SEI shows 15.3% LiF vs 9.2% for PVEC, lower organic C content.

4. **TOF-SIMS**: Demonstrated uniform distribution of inorganic species (LiF₂⁻, PO₂⁻/PO₃⁻) throughout SEI, with thinner organic outer layer compared to PVEC's organic-rich SEI.

5. **Thermal Analysis**: TGA showed reduced residual VEC content (23% vs 54% for PVEC), contributing to non-flammability. ARC tests confirmed superior thermal stability with higher T₁ (160.8°C vs 139.7°C) and T₂ (244.4°C vs 188.9°C).

## Conclusions and Significance

This work presents a revolutionary puzzle-like molecular assembly strategy for constructing multifunctional solid-state polymer electrolytes that simultaneously address the critical challenges of ionic conductivity, Li⁺ transference number, high-voltage stability, and safety in lithium metal batteries. The intelligent combination of VEC, HFBMA, and TAP segments through in-situ polymerization creates a synergistic effect where each component contributes specific functionalities while maintaining overall system compatibility. The achieved ultra-high tLi⁺ (0.70), excellent high-voltage stability (5.15 V), superior non-flammability, and practical performance under demanding conditions (high loading, lean electrolyte, extreme temperatures) represent a significant advancement in solid-state battery technology. The successful demonstration of 349 Wh kg⁻¹ specific energy in a 2.6-Ah pouch cell, combined with exceptional safety performance verified by comprehensive abuse tests, provides a clear pathway for practical implementation of high-energy-density solid-state lithium metal batteries. This work establishes new design principles for advanced polymer electrolytes and accelerates the commercialization of next-generation energy storage systems.

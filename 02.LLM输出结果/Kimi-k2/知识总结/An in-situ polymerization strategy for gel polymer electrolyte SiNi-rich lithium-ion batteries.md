## Research Background and Motivation

This research addresses critical challenges in high-energy-density lithium-ion batteries coupling Si-based anodes with nickel-rich NMC cathodes (x ≥ 0.8). The main problems include: mechanical instability of Li-Si alloys during cycling, cathode structural collapse at high voltages, severe leakage current at elevated temperatures, and detrimental cross-talk effects where transition metal cations migrate from cathode to anode, depleting the active lithium reservoir. These issues severely limit the practical implementation of energy-dense Si||NMC battery systems.

## Research Methods and Innovation Points

### Composition of Gel Electrolyte

| Component | Specific Material | Content/Ratio |
|---------|---------|----------|
| **Polymer Matrix/Monomer** | Poly(vinylene carbonate) (PVCM) | 40% VC volume fraction |
| **Lithium Salt** | Lithium difluoro(oxalato)borate (LiDFOB) | 1 M |
| **Solvent** | Ethylene carbonate/Diethyl carbonate (EC/DEC, 1:1 v/v) | 60% |
| **Additive** | Azobisisobutyronitrile (AIBN) | 0.2 wt% (vs VC) |
| **Initiator/Crosslinking Agent** | Thermal radical initiator (AIBN) | 0.2 wt% |
| **Polymerization Conditions** | 60°C for 24h + 80°C for 2h | In-situ thermal polymerization |

### Polymerization Method

- **Polymerization Type**: In-situ radical polymerization
- **Polymerization Conditions**: 60°C for 24 hours followed by 80°C for 2 hours under argon atmosphere

### Key Innovation Points

1. **Hierarchical Si/C@C Anode Design**: Micron-sized Si nanoparticles uniformly dispersed in coal tar pitch-derived carbon scaffold through spray-drying and pyrolysis, providing mechanical stress dissipation during lithiation.

2. **Non-flammable PVCM-GPE**: In-situ polymerized vinylene carbonate matrix with LiDFOB salt achieving 8.61×10⁻⁴ S cm⁻¹ ionic conductivity at 25°C while maintaining 39.5 MPa tensile strength and self-extinguishing property.

3. **Dual-interface Stabilization**: PVCM polymer and DFOB⁻ anions simultaneously construct stable SEI on Si anode and CEI on NMC cathode, while chelating transition metal cations to suppress cross-talk effects.

## Mechanism Analysis and Characterization

The study employed multiple characterization techniques to elucidate the stabilization mechanisms:

1. **XPS Analysis**: Revealed formation of poly(VC)-rich CEI/SEI layers with LiF, B-O, and B-F species from LiDFOB decomposition, effectively suppressing electrolyte corrosion and transition metal dissolution.

2. **TOF-SIMS Depth Profiling**: Demonstrated homogeneous distribution of organic (C₂HO⁻) and inorganic (LiF₂⁻, BO⁻) species in SEI, with significantly reduced transition metal (Ni, Mn, Co) crossover in GPE system.

3. **ICP-MS Quantification**: Showed 81% reduction in Ni deposition (226→43 ppm) and 77% reduction in Mn deposition (118→27 ppm) on anode surface compared to liquid electrolyte.

4. **COMSOL Simulation**: Finite element modeling revealed 2.1× lower stress accumulation in Si particles and 43× lower compressive stress in carbon layer for Si/C@C|GPE vs Si/G@C|LE systems.

5. **Operando XRD**: Real-time monitoring confirmed negligible lattice parameter changes in NMC811 cathode (Δc = 0.17% vs 0.69% in LE) during cycling, indicating superior structural stability.

## Conclusions and Significance

This work presents a comprehensive solution to the fundamental challenges of high-energy-density Si||NMC lithium-ion batteries through an integrated materials design approach. The hierarchical Si/C@C anode architecture combined with in-situ polymerized PVCM gel polymer electrolyte simultaneously addresses mechanical instability, interfacial degradation, safety concerns, and transition metal cross-talk effects. The resulting 2.7 Ah pouch cell achieves exceptional performance metrics: 325.9 Wh kg⁻¹ energy density, 88.7% capacity retention over 2000 cycles, wide temperature operation (-20 to 60°C), and intrinsic flame retardancy. This study provides crucial insights into multiscale degradation mechanisms and demonstrates a practical pathway toward next-generation energy-dense, safe lithium-ion batteries for electric vehicles and grid storage applications.

## Research Background and Motivation

This research addresses the challenge of forming stable solid-electrolyte interphases (SEI) in Li-metal batteries. While fluorinated reagents improve cycling performance through LiF-rich SEI formation, they pose environmental and cost concerns. The study proposes a fluorine-free quasi-solid polymer electrolyte that generates an O/N-containing hybrid SEI, offering improved interfacial stability and Li+ transport kinetics without the environmental drawbacks of fluorinated systems.

## Research Methods and Innovation Points

### Composition of Gel Electrolyte

| Component | Specific Material | Content/Ratio |
|---------|---------|----------|
| **Polymer Matrix/Monomer** | Vinylene carbonate (VC) + N,N'-methylenebisacrylamide (MBA) | VC:MBA = 100:2 molar ratio |
| **Lithium Salt** | Li bis(oxalate)borate (LiBOB) | 1 mol L⁻¹ |
| **Solvent** | Propylene carbonate (PC) + 1,2-dimethoxyethane (DME) | PC:DME = 1:3 volume ratio |
| **Additive** | Li nitrate (LiNO₃) | 0.2 mol L⁻¹ |
| **Initiator/Crosslinking Agent** | Azodiisobutyronitrile (AIBN) | 0.5 wt% |
| **Polymerization Conditions** | 70°C, 6h, argon atmosphere |

### Polymerization Method

- **Polymerization Type**: In-situ radical copolymerization
- **Polymerization Conditions**: 70°C for 6 hours under argon atmosphere

### Key Innovation Points

1. **Fluorine-free SEI design**: Constructs Li₂O-rich inorganic SEI without fluorinated components
2. **Preferential crystal plane growth**: Directs Li deposition along (200) crystallographic plane rather than (110)
3. **Anion-dominated solvation structure**: BOB⁻ dominates solvation shell, promoting anion-derived SEI formation

## Mechanism Analysis and Characterization

The study employed multiple characterization techniques to reveal the mechanism:

- **XPS depth profiling**: Confirmed Li₂O content of 76% in SEI (vs 28% in PDE)
- **TEM analysis**: Visualized uniform SEI with Li₂O (111) and (200) lattice fringes
- **XRD monitoring**: Demonstrated preferential (200) crystal plane growth during Li deposition
- **DFT calculations**: Showed PVM-GPE has lower surface energy for (200) plane
- **SEM imaging**: Revealed dense, flat Li morphology without dendrites
- **TOF-SIMS**: Confirmed uniform O-containing SEI components

## Conclusions and Significance

This work demonstrates a sustainable fluorine-free SEI design that achieves comparable or superior performance to conventional fluorinated systems. The Li₂O-rich SEI formed by PVM-GPE enables stable Li-metal batteries with 84.7% capacity retention over 800 cycles at 3C. The preferential (200) crystal plane growth and anion-dominated solvation structure provide new insights for designing environmentally friendly, high-performance solid-state batteries. The technology shows practical potential with successful demonstration in 1Ah pouch cells maintaining stable operation under mechanical abuse conditions.

# Upgrading traditional liquid electrolyte via in situ gelation for future lithium metal batteries - Data Extraction

| Component | Material | Content/Conditions |
|-----------|----------|--------------------|
| Polymer Matrix | PDXL (polydioxolane) | In-situ cationic ring-opening of DOL |
| Monomer | DOL (1,3-dioxolane) | 1 : 1 v/v DOL/DME |
| Lithium Salt | LiPF₆ + LiTFSI | 2 M LiPF₆ + 1 M LiTFSI in DOL/DME |
| Lithium Salt Concentration | 2 M LiPF₆ | 2 M LiPF₆ + 1 M LiTFSI |
| Solvent | DOL/DME (1:1 v/v) | Liquid phase before gelation |
| Additives | Trace H₂O (~12 ppm) | Acts as co-initiator |
| Initiator | LiPF₆ (source of PF₅) | Spontaneous PF₅ release at room T |
| Crosslinking Agent | - | Not reported |
| Polymerization Method | Cationic ring-opening | In-situ, ambient temperature |
| Polymerization Conditions | Room temperature, Ar | 10 h for full gelation |

# Bioinspired gel polymer electrolyte for wide temperature lithium metal battery - Data Extraction

## Electrolyte Composition

| Component | Material | Content/Conditions |
|-----------|----------|--------------------|
| Polymer Matrix | PTFMA (poly(2,2,2-trifluoroethyl methacrylate)) | Brush-like framework, 20 wt% |
| Monomer | TFMA (2,2,2-trifluoroethyl methacrylate) | 20 wt% |
| Lithium Salt | LiTFSI (lithium bis(trifluoromethanesulfonyl)imide) | 1 M in FEP |
| Lithium Salt Concentration | 1 M | – |
| Solvent | FEP (ethyl 3,3,3-trifluoropropanoate) | Major solvent |
| Additives | FEC (5 wt%), PEGDA (1 wt%), AIBN (0.5 wt%) | FEC for interface, PEGDA cross-linker, AIBN initiator |
| Initiator | AIBN (2,2′-Azobis(2-methylpropionitrile)) | 0.5 wt% |
| Crosslinking Agent | PEGDA (poly(ethylene glycol) diacrylate) | 1 wt% |
| Polymerization Method | In-situ thermal radical polymerization | 70 °C, 5 h |
| Polymerization Conditions | 70 °C, 5 h, Ar glovebox | – |

| Component | Material | Content/Conditions |
|-----------|----------|--------------------|
| Polymer Matrix | PTMG-MDI-EDA copolymer (copolymer 1) | Soft PTMG (Mw 2900) : MDI-EDA hard segment = 1 : 2 (molar) |

| Lithium Salt | LiPF₆ | 1 M in EC/DEC (1:1 v/v) + 10 wt % FEC |
| Lithium Salt Concentration | 1 M | 1 M LiPF₆ in EC/DEC |
| Solvent | EC/DEC (1:1 v/v) | Liquid electrolyte absorbed by copolymer |
| Additives | FEC | 10 wt % fluoroethylene carbonate |
| Initiator | - | Not reported |
| Crosslinking Agent | - | Not reported |
| Polymerization Method | Step-growth polycondensation | Two-step: 80 °C 4 h → 70 °C 4 h under Ar |
| Polymerization Conditions | 80 °C 4 h + 70 °C 4 h | DMAc solvent, Ar atmosphere |

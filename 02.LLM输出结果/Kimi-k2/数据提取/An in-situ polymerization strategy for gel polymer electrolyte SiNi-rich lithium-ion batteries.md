# An in-situ polymerization strategy for gel polymer electrolyte Si||Ni-rich lithium-ion batteries - Data Extraction

## Electrolyte Composition

| Component | Material | Content/Conditions |
|-----------|----------|--------------------|
| Polymer Matrix | PVCM (poly-vinylene carbonate) | 40 vol% VC in EC/DEC |
| Monomer | VC (vinylene carbonate) | 40 vol% VC |
| Lithium Salt | LiDFOB | 1 M |
| Lithium Salt Concentration | 1 M | in VC:EC/DEC (2:3 v/v) |
| Solvent | EC/DEC (1:1 v/v) | 60 vol% |
| Additives | AIBN (0.2 wt%) | Thermal initiator |
| Initiator | AIBN (azobisisobutyronitrile) | 0.2 wt% vs VC |
| Crosslinking Agent | None (linear polymer) | – |
| Polymerization Method | In-situ thermal radical polymerization | 60 °C 24 h + 80 °C 2 h |
| Polymerization Conditions | 60 °C 24 h, then 80 °C 2 h, Ar glovebox | – |

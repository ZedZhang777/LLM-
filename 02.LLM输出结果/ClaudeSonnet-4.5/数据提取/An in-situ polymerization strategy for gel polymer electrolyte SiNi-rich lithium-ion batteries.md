|-----------|----------|--------------------|
| Polymer Matrix | PVCM (Poly(vinylene carbonate) matrix) | Formed via in-situ polymerization |

| Lithium Salt | LiDFOB (Lithium difluoro(oxalate) borate) | 1 M |
| Lithium Salt Concentration | 1 M | In VC:EC/DEC solution |
| Solvent | EC/DEC (Ethylene carbonate/Diethyl carbonate) | 1:1 v/v ratio |
| Additives | Not reported | Not reported |
| Initiator | AIBN (Azobisisobutyronitrile) | 0.2 wt% (vs VC) |
| Crosslinking Agent | Not reported | Not reported |
| Polymerization Method | In-situ radical polymerization | Thermal polymerization |
| Polymerization Conditions | 60°C for 24h, then 80°C for 2h | Two-step heating process |

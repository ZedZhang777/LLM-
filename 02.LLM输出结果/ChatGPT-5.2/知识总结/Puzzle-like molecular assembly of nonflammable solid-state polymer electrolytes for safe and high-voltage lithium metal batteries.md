## Research Background and Motivation

Conventional liquid electrolytes suffer from flammability and poor interfacial stability with lithium metal, leading to severe safety risks and limited high-voltage operation. Solid polymer electrolytes improve safety but often exhibit low ionic conductivity and insufficient mechanical strength. Therefore, designing a non-flammable solid-state polymer electrolyte that simultaneously achieves high conductivity, mechanical robustness, and interfacial stability is critical for safe, high-energy lithium metal batteries.

### Polymerization Method

* **Polymerization Type**: In-situ radical polymerization
* **Polymerization Conditions**: Thermal initiation at 60 °C for 12 h inside the assembled cell

### Key Innovation Points

1. Introduction of a “puzzle-like” molecular assembly strategy integrating rigid and flexible polymer segments to balance mechanical strength and ionic conductivity.
2. Construction of a non-flammable solid-state polymer electrolyte using TMP-based plasticization without sacrificing electrochemical stability.
3. Formation of a stable polymer/Li interphase enabling high-voltage (>4.5 V) lithium metal batteries with long cycling life.

### Special Performance

* **Wide-Temperature Performance**: Maintains >10⁻⁴ S cm⁻¹ conductivity at sub-zero temperatures.
* **Safety**: Exhibits intrinsic non-flammability due to phosphate-based solvent and crosslinked polymer network.
* **Other Characteristic Performance**: High-voltage compatibility up to 5.0 V with suppressed electrolyte decomposition.

## Mechanism Analysis and Characterization

XPS and Raman spectroscopy reveal strong coordination between Li⁺ and ether/phosphate groups, forming continuous ion-transport pathways. SEM and TEM confirm homogeneous polymer networks without phase separation. Molecular dynamics simulations demonstrate that the rigid–flexible “puzzle-like” assembly reduces polymer crystallinity and lowers Li⁺ migration barriers, accounting for the high conductivity and interfacial stability.

## Conclusions and Significance

This work proposes a puzzle-like molecular assembly strategy to construct a non-flammable solid-state polymer electrolyte that simultaneously achieves high ionic conductivity, mechanical robustness, and wide electrochemical stability. The electrolyte enables safe, long-life, high-voltage lithium metal batteries, providing a viable design paradigm for next-generation solid-state energy storage systems.

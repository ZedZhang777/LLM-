## Research Background and Motivation
This research aims to solve the fundamental challenge of developing stable solid-electrolyte interphases (SEI) for Li-metal batteries. Although fluorinated reagents improve cycling by forming LiF-rich SEI, their high cost and environmental impact are problematic. Current fluorine-free polymer electrolyte systems (e.g., based on PMMA, PEO) suffer from narrow electrochemical windows, poor compatibility with high-voltage cathodes, and insufficient research on SEI composition, leading to poor battery cycling. This work is necessary to develop a high-performance, sustainable fluorine-free gel polymer electrolyte (GPE) that can form a stable, Li₂O-rich SEI, offering a viable alternative to fluorinated systems.

## Research Methods and Innovation Points

### Composition of Gel Electrolyte

| Component | Specific Material | Content/Ratio |
|---------|---------|----------|
| **Polymer Matrix/Monomer** | Vinylene Carbonate (VC), N,N’-methylenebisacrylamide (MBA) | MBA at 2% molar ratio to VC |
| **Lithium Salt** | Li bis(oxalate)borate (LiBOB) | 1.0 mol L⁻¹ |
| **Solvent** | Propylene Carbonate (PC) / 1,2-Dimethoxyethane (DME) | Volume ratio 1:3 |
| **Additive** | Li nitrate (LiNO₃) | 0.2 mol L⁻¹ |
| **Initiator/Crosslinking Agent** | Azobisisobutyronitrile (AIBN) | 0.5 wt% |
| **Polymerization Conditions** | 70 °C, 6 h, Ar atmosphere |

### Polymerization Method
- **Polymerization Type**: In-situ Radical Copolymerization
- **Polymerization Conditions**: Temperature: 70°C, Time: 6 h, Initiation Method: Thermal initiation by AIBN.

### Key Innovation Points
1. **Design of a Fluorine-free, Li₂O-rich SEI:** The PVM-GPE induces the formation of a sustainable SEI layer rich in inorganic Li₂O and Li₃N, replacing the conventional LiF-rich SEI.
2. **Regulation of Li Deposition Crystallographic Orientation:** The formed SEI preferentially directs Li metal deposition along the (200) plane instead of the (110) plane, suppressing dendrite growth and enhancing interfacial stability.
3. **Enhanced Li⁺ Transport Dynamics:** The cross-linked polymer matrix (PVM), through hydrogen bonding and weak solvation, promotes an anion-dominated solvation structure, achieving a high Li⁺ transference number (tLi⁺ = 0.77).

### Special Performance
- **Wide-Temperature Performance**: The PVM-GPE has a low Tg (-34°C), indicating good low-temperature potential. High-temperature performance up to 100°C was tested for conductivity.
- **Safety**: Pouch cells passed abuse tests (bending, folding, cutting) and remained functional. The fluorine-free composition avoids HF generation and related hazards.
- **Other Characteristic Performance**: Enables high-rate (up to 5C) and high-voltage (up to 4.5V) cycling with NCM811 and NCM90 cathodes. Pouch cell achieved stable cycling for >150 cycles with high capacity.

## Mechanism Analysis and Characterization
- **MD Simulations & Raman Spectroscopy**: Revealed that PVM-GPE promotes an anion (BOB⁻)-dominated solvation structure, reducing solvent coordination and forming more Contact Ion Pairs (CIPs), which facilitates Li⁺ desolvation and transport.
- **XPS Depth Profiling & TOF-SIMS**: Quantitatively confirmed the SEI is rich in inorganic Li₂O (76% in the outer layer) and Li₃N. Oxygen content increased with sputtering depth, confirming Li₂O as a primary inner SEI component.
- **HR-TEM & SADE**: Directly visualized a uniform SEI layer and identified lattice fringes corresponding to Li₂O (111) and (200) planes, proving its crystalline presence within the SEI.
- **Ex-situ XRD**: Demonstrated that PVM-GPE SEI modulates Li deposition from the typical (110) plane to the (200) plane, which has lower surface energy, leading to more planar and uniform growth.
- **DFT Calculations**: Showed strong binding energy between Li⁺ and the PVM polymer chain, explaining the high tLi⁺. Also calculated lower adsorption energy of PVM on the Li(200) plane, corroborating the preferred orientation.

## Conclusions and Significance
This work successfully developed a sustainable, fluorine-free quasi-solid polymer electrolyte (PVM-GPE) via in-situ polymerization. Its core achievement is constructing a robust, Li₂O/Li₃N-rich SEI that regulates Li⁺ solvation, promotes preferential Li deposition on the (200) plane, and enables fast Li⁺ transport (high tLi⁺ of 0.77). Consequently, Li-metal batteries using PVM-GPE exhibit exceptional interfacial stability (1500 h in symmetric cells), high-rate capability (84.7% retention after 800 cycles at 3C), and compatibility with high-voltage/high-nickel cathodes (up to 4.5V). This study provides a novel, environmentally benign materials design paradigm, moving away from fluorinated components, and demonstrates significant potential for the development of high-performance, sustainable quasi-solid-state lithium metal batteries.
